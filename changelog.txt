Version: 0.1.2
Date: 2026-11-21
  Bugfixes:
    - Real last-meter logic: when the route is consumed (or a straight-line
      order), the tick loop re-asserts the goal every time the autopilot
      destination lapses or the spider stops short: a genuine
      brake-and-settle approach instead of mid-flight pass-throughs that
      burned the old 3-assert budget. Bounded at 6, then the stall
      watchdog repaths as before. Covers waypoint routes and <20-tile
      straight-line orders alike.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.1
Date: 2026-11-21
  Bugfixes:
    - Fast spiders no longer coast past the final waypoint: when the route's
      last node is consumed the goal is re-asserted (bounded, up to 3 times)
      so the autopilot performs a fresh brake-and-settle onto it instead of
      overshooting. Stall-watchdog path uses the same settle re-assert before
      repathing.
